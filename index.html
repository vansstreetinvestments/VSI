Module 01 • Order Book Physics

Market Microstructure & Point Processes

Mutually Exciting Hawkes Point Processes

Formulates clustered arrivals of market orders, cancellations, and liquidity consumption where historical events excite future arrival intensities.

$$\lambda_i(t) = \mu_i + \sum_{j=1}^M \int_0^t \alpha_{ij} e^{-\beta_{ij}(t-s)} dN_j(s)$$
• $\mu_i$: Exogenous baseline Poisson intensity
• $\alpha_{ij}$: Cross-excitation jump amplitude
• $\beta_{ij}$: Exponential memory decay rate
• Stability: Requires spectral radius $\rho(\mathbf{\Gamma}) < 1$ with $\Gamma_{ij} = \frac{\alpha_{ij}}{\beta_{ij}}$

Stoikov's Micro-Price & Queue Dynamics

Computes the volume-weighted martingale price reflecting queue size asymmetry between best bid ($Q^b$) and best ask ($Q^a$) before next mid-price jump.

$$P_{\text{micro}} = \frac{Q^b}{Q^b + Q^a} P^a + \frac{Q^a}{Q^b + Q^a} P^b = P_{\text{mid}} + \frac{S}{2} \mathcal{I}_Q$$
• $\mathcal{I}_Q = \frac{Q^b - Q^a}{Q^b + Q^a}$: Normalized queue volume imbalance $\in [-1, +1]$
• $S = P^a - P^b$: Instantaneous bid-ask spread
• Martingale Property: $\mathbb{E}[P_{\tau} \mid \mathcal{F}_t] = P_{\text{micro}}(t)$ under Markov jumps

Kyle's Lambda & Adverse Selection

Formulates equilibrium market depth when market makers clear aggregate order flow composed of informed insider orders and exogenous noise trades.

$$\Delta P_t = \lambda (x_t + u_t), \quad \lambda = \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\frac{\Sigma_0}{\sigma_u^2}}$$
• $\lambda$: Kyle's Lambda (price impact per unit volume)
• $\Sigma_0$: Prior uncertainty regarding asset fundamental value
• $\sigma_u^2$: Variance of uninformed noise trading

Bouchaud Non-Linear Transient Impact Propagator

Resolves persistent sign correlations in order flow without leading to arbitrage via a decaying power-law propagator kernel $G(\tau)$.

$$I(t) = \int_0^t G(t - s) \epsilon(s) ds, \quad G(\tau) \sim \tau^{-\gamma} \quad (0.3 \le \gamma \le 0.6)$$
• $\epsilon(s) \in \{-1, +1\}$: Trade sign sequence
• $G(\tau)$: Bare impact propagator quantifying memory decay
• Square-Root Law: Aggregate impact $I \propto \sigma \sqrt{Q/V}$
Module 02 • Stochastic Optimal Control

Optimal Execution & Market Making Models

Avellaneda-Stoikov Market Making (2008)

Maximizes expected terminal utility under CARA utility by deriving reservation price $r(s,q,t)$ and optimal quoting spreads.

$$r(s, q, t) = s - q \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t)$$ $$\delta^a + \delta^b = \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t) + \frac{2}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right)$$
• $r(s, q, t)$: Indifference price for marginal inventory unit
• $\delta^a, \delta^b$: Distance of quotes from mid price $s$
• Fill Intensity: $\lambda(\delta) = A e^{-\kappa \delta}$

Almgren-Chriss Optimal Liquidation (2000)

Constructs the efficient execution frontier by minimizing the mean-variance trade-off between market volatility and execution impact.

$$x_j = \frac{\sinh(\kappa(T - t_j))}{\sinh(\kappa T)} X_0, \quad \kappa = \text{arcosh}\left(\frac{\tau^2 \lambda \sigma^2}{2\eta} + 1\right)$$
• $x_j$: Remaining inventory shares at step $t_j$
• $\eta$: Temporary price impact parameter
• $\lambda$: Risk-aversion penalty coefficient
Module 03 • Stochastic Differential Equations

Continuous-Time Stochastic Calculus

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Diffusion

The foundational stationary Gauss-Markov process modeling cross-asset spreads, volatility drift, and basis spreads with linear restoring pull.

$$dX_t = \theta (\mu - X_t) dt + \sigma dW_t$$ $$\mathbb{V}\text{ar}[X_\infty] = \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta}, \quad \tau_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\theta}$$
• $\theta$: Mean-reversion velocity
• $\mu$: Unconditional equilibrium level
• Half-Life $\tau_{1/2}$: Expected duration to eliminate 50% of displacement

Multidimensional Itô's Lemma

The fundamental chain rule of stochastic analysis accounting for non-zero quadratic variation of Brownian motion: $(dW_t)^2 = dt$.

$$df = \left( \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + \boldsymbol{\mu}^T \nabla f + \frac{1}{2} \text{Tr}\left(\boldsymbol{\Sigma} \nabla^2 f\right) \right) dt + (\nabla f)^T \boldsymbol{\sigma} d\mathbf{W}_t$$
• Itô Drift Correction: $\frac{1}{2} \sigma^2 S^2 \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2} dt$ generates option Gamma
• Quadratic Variation: $[W, W]_t = t$ almost surely
Module 04 • Implied Volatility Geometry

Volatility Surfaces & Derivatives Models

Black-Scholes PDE & Cross-Greeks

Constructs the risk-neutral valuation PDE and higher-order sensitivities critical for institutional dynamic delta-gamma-vega hedging.

$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 \Gamma + r S \Delta - r V = 0$$ $$\text{Vanna} = \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S \partial \sigma} = -\phi(d_1) \frac{d_2}{\sigma}, \quad \text{Volga} = \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \sigma^2} = \mathcal{V} \frac{d_1 d_2}{\sigma}$$
• Vanna: Delta sensitivity to IV; key for skew hedging
• Volga (Vomma): Vega sensitivity to IV; measures curvature risk

Heston Stochastic Volatility Model (1993)

Models continuous variance $v_t$ as a mean-reverting CIR process correlated with spot price $S_t$ generating volatility smiles.

$$dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sqrt{v_t} S_t dW_t^S$$ $$dv_t = \kappa(\theta - v_t)dt + \xi \sqrt{v_t} dW_t^v, \quad \mathbb{E}[dW_t^S dW_t^v] = \rho dt$$
• Feller Condition: $2\kappa\theta > \xi^2$ guarantees variance $v_t > 0$ strictly
• Correlation $\rho < 0$: Produces downward equity volatility skew
• Vol-of-vol $\xi$: Controls curvature of volatility smile wings
Module 05 • Recursive State Estimation

State-Space Filtering & Latent Regimes

Discrete Linear Kalman Filter

The minimum mean squared error (MMSE) estimator for dynamic latent states (e.g., dynamic hedge ratio $\beta_t$ in cointegrated pairs).

$$\mathbf{K}_k = \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T \left(\mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T + \mathbf{R}_k\right)^{-1}$$ $$\mathbf{x}_k = \mathbf{x}_k^- + \mathbf{K}_k (\mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{x}_k^-), \quad \mathbf{P}_k = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{H}_k)\mathbf{P}_k^-$$
• Innovation: $\mathbf{y}_k = \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{x}_k^-$ (Residual error)
• $\mathbf{K}_k$: Optimal Kalman Gain balancing prior vs new measurement
• $\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{R}$: Process noise and measurement noise covariance

Hidden Markov Models (HMM) & Viterbi Paths

Infers unobserved regime states $S_t \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ (e.g., Bull Trending, Mean-Reverting, Volatility Shock).

$$V_{t,k} = \max_{i} \left( V_{t-1, i} \cdot A_{ik} \right) \cdot B_k(y_t)$$
• Matrix $\mathbf{A}$: State transition probabilities between regimes
• Emission $B_k(y)$: Probability of return $y_t$ given state $k$
• Viterbi Algorithm: Dynamic programming decoding of most probable state path
Module 06 • Heavy Tails & Systemic Risk

Quantitative Risk, Extremes & Cointegration

Extreme Value Theory (EVT) & Peaks-Over-Threshold

By the Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem, excess losses beyond high threshold $u$ converge to the Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD).

$$G_{\xi, \beta}(y) = 1 - \left( 1 + \frac{\xi y}{\beta} \right)^{-1/\xi} \quad (y = x - u > 0)$$ $$\text{CVaR}_\alpha = \frac{\text{VaR}_\alpha}{1 - \xi} + \frac{\beta - \xi u}{1 - \xi}$$
• Shape $\xi > 0$: Fréchet heavy-tailed financial returns
• Scale $\beta$: Tail volatility scale above threshold $u$
• Coherent Risk: Expected Shortfall (CVaR) satisfies subadditivity

Copula Theory & Tail Crash Dependency

By Sklar's Theorem, decomposes joint distributions into marginals and copula $C$ to measure systemic tail crash correlation.

$$F(\mathbf{x}) = C(u_1, \dots, u_d), \quad \lambda_L = 2 t_{\nu+1}\left( -\sqrt{\frac{(\nu+1)(1-\rho)}{1+\rho}} \right)$$
• Student-t Copula: Exhibits lower tail dependence $\lambda_L > 0$ during crashes
• Gaussian Copula: Asymptotically tail-independent ($\lambda_L = 0$); understates systemic risk
