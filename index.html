Module 01 • Order Book Physics

Market Microstructure & Point Processes

High-frequency price formation, continuous double auctions, order flow imbalance, and self-exciting point processes.

Mutually Exciting Hawkes Point Processes

High-Frequency

Formulates clustered arrivals of market orders, limit order book cancellations, and liquidity consumption where historical events instantaneously excite future arrival probabilities.

$$\lambda_i(t) = \mu_i + \sum_{j=1}^M \int_0^t \alpha_{ij} e^{-\beta_{ij}(t-s)} dN_j(s)$$
• $\mu_i$: Exogenous baseline Poisson intensity
• $\alpha_{ij}$: Cross-excitation jump amplitude from event stream $j$ to $i$
• $\beta_{ij}$: Exponential memory decay rate
• Spectral Radius: Stability requires $\rho(\mathbf{\Gamma}) < 1$ where $\Gamma_{ij} = \frac{\alpha_{ij}}{\beta_{ij}}$

Stoikov's Micro-Price & Queue Dynamics

Microstructure

Computes the volume-weighted martingale price reflecting queue size asymmetry between best bid ($Q^b$) and best ask ($Q^a$) before next mid-price transition.

$$P_{\text{micro}} = \frac{Q^b}{Q^b + Q^a} P^a + \frac{Q^a}{Q^b + Q^a} P^b = P_{\text{mid}} + \frac{S}{2} \mathcal{I}_Q$$
• $\mathcal{I}_Q = \frac{Q^b - Q^a}{Q^b + Q^a}$: Normalized queue volume imbalance $\in [-1, +1]$
• $S = P^a - P^b$: Instantaneous bid-ask spread
• Martingale Property: $\mathbb{E}[P_{\tau} \mid \mathcal{F}_t] = P_{\text{micro}}(t)$ under Markov jump assumptions

Kyle's Lambda & Adverse Selection

Information Theory

Formulates equilibrium market depth when market makers clear aggregate order flow composed of informed insider orders and exogenous noise trades.

$$\Delta P_t = \lambda (x_t + u_t), \quad \lambda = \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\frac{\Sigma_0}{\sigma_u^2}}$$
• $\lambda$: Kyle's Lambda (price impact per unit of net traded volume)
• $\Sigma_0$: Prior uncertainty regarding asset fundamental value $v \sim \mathcal{N}(p_0, \Sigma_0)$
• $\sigma_u^2$: Variance of uninformed liquidity noise trading

Bouchaud Non-Linear Transient Impact Propagator

Market Impact

Resolves the puzzle of persistent sign correlations in order flow without leading to arbitrage via a decaying power-law propagator kernel $G(\tau)$.

$$I(t) = \int_0^t G(t - s) \epsilon(s) ds, \quad G(\tau) \sim \tau^{-\gamma} \quad (0.3 \le \gamma \le 0.6)$$
• $\epsilon(s) \in \{-1, +1\}$: Trade sign sequence (buyer vs seller initiated)
• $G(\tau)$: Bare impact propagator quantifying memory decay
• Square-Root Law: Yields aggregate impact $I \propto \sigma \sqrt{\frac{Q}{V}}$ at meta-order scale
Module 02 • Stochastic Optimal Control

Optimal Execution & Market Making Models

Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, inventory risk penalty, and liquidation trajectories.

Avellaneda-Stoikov Market Making (2008)

Stochastic Control

Maximizes expected terminal utility under constant absolute risk aversion (CARA) by deriving reservation price $r(s,q,t)$ and optimal quoting spreads.

$$r(s, q, t) = s - q \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t)$$ $$\delta^a + \delta^b = \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t) + \frac{2}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right)$$
• $r(s, q, t)$: Indifference price for marginal inventory unit
• $\delta^a, \delta^b$: Distance of Ask and Bid quotes from mid price $s$
• Fill Intensity: $\lambda(\delta) = A e^{-\kappa \delta}$

Almgren-Chriss Optimal Liquidation (2000)

Calculus of Variations

Constructs the efficient execution frontier by minimizing the mean-variance trade-off between market risk volatility and permanent/temporary market friction.

$$x_j = \frac{\sinh(\kappa(T - t_j))}{\sinh(\kappa T)} X_0, \quad \kappa = \text{arcosh}\left(\frac{\tau^2 \lambda \sigma^2}{2\eta} + 1\right)$$
• $x_j$: Remaining inventory shares at time step $t_j$
• $\eta$: Temporary price impact parameter ($h(v) = \eta v$)
• $\lambda$: Risk-aversion penalty coefficient ($\mathbb{E}[x] + \lambda \mathbb{V}\text{ar}[x]$)

Guéant-Tapia-Manziadi Closed-Form Transformation

Asymptotic Analysis

Provides closed-form approximations linearizing the coupled Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman PDEs in high-frequency order placement with finite horizon $T$.

$$\delta^a(q) = \frac{1}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right) + \frac{(2q + 1) \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t)}{2}$$ $$\delta^b(q) = \frac{1}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right) - \frac{(2q - 1) \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t)}{2}$$
• Linear Decoupling: Asymmetry increases linearly with current inventory state $q$
• Total Spread: Constant across inventory states: $S^* = \frac{2}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right) + \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t)$

Cartea-Jaimungal Drift-Aware Execution

Short-Term Alpha

Extends execution models to incorporate predictive short-term alpha signals $\alpha_t$ (e.g., book imbalance or latency arbitrage signals) into quote placement.

$$v_t^* = v_t^{\text{passive}} + \frac{1}{\eta} \int_t^T \frac{\sinh(\kappa(s - t))}{\sinh(\kappa(T - t))} \mathbb{E}_t[\alpha_s] ds$$
• Predictive Drift: $\alpha_s = \mathbb{E}_t[dS_s/ds]$ accelerates or retards liquidation speed
• Favorable Alpha ($\alpha > 0$): Slows down liquidation to capture upward capital drift
Module 03 • Stochastic Differential Equations

Continuous-Time Stochastic Calculus

Itô calculus, diffusion processes, Fokker-Planck probability density propagation, and Girsanov measure shifts.

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Diffusion

Mean Reversion

The foundational stationary Gauss-Markov process modeling cross-asset spreads, volatility drift, and interest rates with linear restoring pull.

$$dX_t = \theta (\mu - X_t) dt + \sigma dW_t$$ $$\mathbb{V}\text{ar}[X_\infty] = \frac{\sigma^2}{2\theta}, \quad \tau_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\theta}$$
• $\theta$: Mean-reversion velocity (stiffness of restoring spring)
• $\mu$: Unconditional equilibrium level
• Half-Life $\tau_{1/2}$: Expected duration to eliminate 50% of displacement

Multidimensional Itô's Lemma

Stochastic Calculus

The fundamental chain rule of stochastic analysis accounting for non-zero quadratic variation of Brownian motion: $(dW_t)^2 = dt$.

$$df = \left( \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + \boldsymbol{\mu}^T \nabla f + \frac{1}{2} \text{Tr}\left(\boldsymbol{\Sigma} \nabla^2 f\right) \right) dt + (\nabla f)^T \boldsymbol{\sigma} d\mathbf{W}_t$$
• Itô Drift Correction: $\frac{1}{2} \sigma^2 S^2 \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2} dt$ generates option convexity (Gamma)
• Quadratic Variation: $[W, W]_t = t$ almost surely

Fokker-Planck (Kolmogorov Forward) Equation

Density Dynamics

Governs the deterministic deterministic evolution of the transition probability density function $p(x,t)$ for general Itô diffusions.

$$\frac{\partial p}{\partial t} = -\frac{\partial}{\partial x} [\mu(x,t) p] + \frac{1}{2} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2} [\sigma^2(x,t) p]$$
• Advection Term: $-\partial_x [\mu p]$ describes deterministic flow along drift vector
• Diffusion Term: $\frac{1}{2}\partial_{xx} [\sigma^2 p]$ describes Brownian dispersion
• Stationary Density: $p_\infty(x) = \lim_{t \to \infty} p(x,t)$ solves $\partial_t p = 0$

Girsanov's Theorem & Risk-Neutral Measure

Measure Theory

Eliminates real-world market risk drift $\mu$ in favor of risk-free rate $r$ via the exponential martingale Radon-Nikodym derivative.

$$\frac{d\mathbb{Q}}{d\mathbb{P}} = \mathcal{E}\left( -\int_0^t \frac{\mu - r}{\sigma} dW_s^{\mathbb{P}} \right), \quad W_t^{\mathbb{Q}} = W_t^{\mathbb{P}} + \int_0^t \frac{\mu - r}{\sigma} ds$$
• Market Price of Risk: $\gamma_s = \frac{\mu - r}{\sigma}$ (Sharpe ratio of asset)
• First Fundamental Theorem: Absence of arbitrage $\iff$ existence of equivalent martingale measure $\mathbb{Q}$
Module 04 • Implied Volatility Geometry

Volatility Surfaces & Derivatives Models

Black-Scholes PDEs, Greeks, Dupire local volatility reconstruction, Heston stochastic variance, and SABR asymptotic expansions.

Black-Scholes-Merton PDE & Cross-Greeks

Derivatives

Constructs the risk-neutral valuation PDE and higher-order sensitivities critical for institutional dynamic delta-gamma-vega hedging.

$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 \Gamma + r S \Delta - r V = 0$$ $$\text{Vanna} = \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S \partial \sigma} = -\phi(d_1) \frac{d_2}{\sigma}, \quad \text{Volga} = \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \sigma^2} = \mathcal{V} \frac{d_1 d_2}{\sigma}$$
• Vanna: Sensitivity of Delta to changes in IV; critical for skew hedging
• Volga (Vomma): Sensitivity of Vega to IV; measures curvature risk of vol positions

Heston Stochastic Volatility Model (1993)

Two-Factor SDE

Models continuous variance $v_t$ as a mean-reverting CIR process correlated with spot price $S_t$ to naturally generate volatility smiles and heavy tails.

$$dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sqrt{v_t} S_t dW_t^S$$ $$dv_t = \kappa(\theta - v_t)dt + \xi \sqrt{v_t} dW_t^v, \quad \mathbb{E}[dW_t^S dW_t^v] = \rho dt$$
• Feller Condition: $2\kappa\theta > \xi^2$ guarantees variance $v_t > 0$ strictly
• Correlation $\rho < 0$: Produces downward equity implied vol skew (leverage effect)
• Vol-of-vol $\xi$: Controls the convexity of the volatility smile wings

Dupire's Local Volatility Equation (1994)

Surface Inversion

Extracts the unique deterministic state-dependent diffusion coefficient $\sigma_{\text{loc}}(K,T)$ consistent with market-observed vanilla European option prices.

$$\sigma_{\text{loc}}^2(K, T) = \frac{\frac{\partial C}{\partial T} + (r - q)K \frac{\partial C}{\partial K} + q C}{\frac{1}{2} K^2 \frac{\partial^2 C}{\partial K^2}}$$
• Numerator: Calendar theta and strike drift adjustments
• Denominator: Breeden-Litzenberger risk-neutral density $\frac{\partial^2 C}{\partial K^2}$
• No-Arbitrage: Requires $\frac{\partial^2 C}{\partial K^2} > 0$ and $\frac{\partial C}{\partial T} \ge 0$

Yang-Zhang Drift-Independent Realized Volatility

High-Frequency Estimation

The minimum-variance unbiased volatility estimator that handles overnight price jumps, continuous open-to-close drift, and intra-candle extrema.

$$\sigma_{YZ}^2 = \sigma_{\text{overnight}}^2 + k \sigma_{\text{open-close}}^2 + (1 - k) \sigma_{\text{Rogers-Satchell}}^2$$
• Overnight Jump: $\sigma_o^2 = \frac{1}{N-1}\sum(\ln(O_i/C_{i-1}) - \bar{o})^2$
• Rogers-Satchell: $\sigma_{RS}^2 = \frac{1}{N}\sum [\ln(H/C)\ln(H/O) + \ln(L/C)\ln(L/O)]$
• Efficiency: Up to $14\times$ more statistically efficient than standard close-to-close variance
Module 05 • Recursive State Estimation

State-Space Filtering & Latent Regimes

Kalman filtering, dynamic hedge ratio tracking, innovation covariance, and Hidden Markov regime switching models.

Discrete Linear Kalman Filter

Recursive Estimation

The minimum mean squared error (MMSE) estimator for dynamic latent states (e.g., time-varying beta $\beta_t$ in statistical pairs trading).

$$\mathbf{K}_k = \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T \left(\mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{P}_k^- \mathbf{H}_k^T + \mathbf{R}_k\right)^{-1}$$ $$\mathbf{x}_k = \mathbf{x}_k^- + \mathbf{K}_k (\mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{x}_k^-), \quad \mathbf{P}_k = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{K}_k \mathbf{H}_k)\mathbf{P}_k^-$$
• Innovation: $\mathbf{y}_k = \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{H}_k \mathbf{x}_k^-$ (Residual prediction error)
• $\mathbf{K}_k$: Optimal Kalman Gain balancing model prior against new noisy measurement
• $\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{R}$: Process noise and measurement noise covariance matrices

Hidden Markov Models (HMM) & Viterbi Paths

Regime Decoding

Infers unobserved macroeconomic and liquidity states $S_t \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ (e.g., Bull Trending, Volatile Mean-Reverting, Liquidity Shock).

$$V_{t,k} = \max_{i} \left( V_{t-1, i} \cdot A_{ik} \right) \cdot B_k(y_t)$$
• Transition Matrix $\mathbf{A}$: State transition probabilities between regimes
• Emission PDF $B_k(y)$: Probability of observing return $y_t$ given regime state $k$
• Viterbi Algorithm: Dynamic programming decoding of most probable hidden path $S_{1:T}^*$
Module 06 • Heavy Tails & Non-Linear Dependency

Quantitative Risk, Extremes & Cointegration

Extreme Value Theory (EVT), Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem, Copula tail dependency, and Johansen cointegration.

Extreme Value Theory (EVT) & Peaks-Over-Threshold

Tail Risk

By the Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem, excess losses beyond high threshold $u$ asymptotically converge to the Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD).

$$G_{\xi, \beta}(y) = 1 - \left( 1 + \frac{\xi y}{\beta} \right)^{-1/\xi} \quad (y = x - u > 0)$$ $$\text{CVaR}_\alpha = \frac{\text{VaR}_\alpha}{1 - \xi} + \frac{\beta - \xi u}{1 - \xi}$$
• Shape Parameter $\xi > 0$: Fréchet heavy-tailed distribution (financial asset returns)
• Scale Parameter $\beta$: Tail volatility scale above threshold $u$
• Coherent Risk: Expected Shortfall (CVaR) satisfies subadditivity unlike VaR

Copula Theory & Joint Crash Dependency

Multivariate Dependence

By Sklar's Theorem, separates marginal return distributions from non-linear multivariate dependence to measure systemic tail crash correlation.

$$F(\mathbf{x}) = C(u_1, \dots, u_d), \quad \lambda_L = 2 t_{\nu+1}\left( -\sqrt{\frac{(\nu+1)(1-\rho)}{1+\rho}} \right)$$
• Student-t Copula: Exhibits non-zero lower tail dependence $\lambda_L > 0$ during market crashes
• Gaussian Copula: Asymptotically tail-independent ($\lambda_L = 0$ as $u \to 0$); dangerously underestimates contagion
• Clayton Copula: Asymmetric lower-tail Archimedean copula: $C(u,v) = (u^{-\theta} + v^{-\theta} - 1)^{-1/\theta}$

Johansen Multilateral Cointegration (VECM)

Statistical Arbitrage

Identifies true stationary linear combinations $\boldsymbol{\beta}^T \mathbf{Y}_t \sim I(0)$ across $N$ integrated $I(1)$ price series via maximum likelihood eigenvalue decomposition.

$$\Delta \mathbf{Y}_t = \boldsymbol{\alpha} \boldsymbol{\beta}^T \mathbf{Y}_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} \boldsymbol{\Gamma}_i \Delta \mathbf{Y}_{t-i} + \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_t$$ $$\lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^k \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i)$$
• Rank $r = \text{rank}(\boldsymbol{\Pi})$: Number of stationary cointegrating vectors
• $\boldsymbol{\beta}$: Matrix of stationary cointegration coefficients
• $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$: Speed-of-adjustment error correction parameter matrix

Expected Shortfall & Spectral Risk Measures

Portfolio Risk

Replaces non-subadditive Value at Risk (VaR) with coherent Conditional VaR (Expected Shortfall) measuring conditional expected loss in the tail.

$$\text{ES}_\alpha(L) = \min_{\zeta \in \mathbb{R}} \left\{ \zeta + \frac{1}{1 - \alpha} \mathbb{E}\left[ (L - \zeta)^+ \right] \right\}$$
• Rockafellar-Uryasev Optimization: Converts non-linear tail optimization into convex linear programming
• Subadditivity Axiom: $\rho(X + Y) \le \rho(X) + \rho(Y)$ strictly guarantees diversification benefits
